## The product
KinnectAI is a subscription-based SaaS platform that brings content creation and image editing tools into one application.

## What I built
- Integrated Gemini and Clipdrop APIs for automated content creation and advanced image editing.
- Implemented secure, role-based access with Clerk.
- Integrated Stripe for tiered memberships and recurring payments.

## Why this matters for clients
This project brings together the core building blocks of a paid SaaS product: an interactive interface, AI integrations, secure access, and subscription billing.
