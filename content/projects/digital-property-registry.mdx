## The product
At Kripanidhi Legal Services, I built and deployed a Digital Property Registry Platform serving more than 500 users.

## What I built
- Multilingual document generation and automated PDF/DOCX output.
- Secure authentication, role-based access, and cloud storage.
- Scalable REST APIs, database schemas, and document-processing workflows.
- Payments, OTP registration, and notification services using Redis and BullMQ.
- Template-driven registry generation and a Docker/AWS deployment lifecycle.

## Client value
This work connects business workflows, documents, payments, and secure access in one production system. It reflects the kind of end-to-end engineering I bring to client projects.
