## The product
BINKEYIT is a grocery e-commerce platform inspired by Blinkit's functionality, built with MongoDB, Express, React, Node.js, and Redux.

## What I built
- Secure user authentication with JWT.
- Password recovery flows.
- Payment integration to support the shopping experience.

## Why this matters for clients
A commerce product needs more than a storefront. Accounts, recovery flows, payments, and the backend must work together to make the experience dependable.
